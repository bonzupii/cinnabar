## Where the design landed

**Settled.** Palette — dark-first, near-black ground `#100E0D`, panels `#171514`,
hairlines `#302C2A`, greys `#7C7570` / `#A29B96` / `#C9C2BD`, text `#EDE9E6`,
vermilion `#E0442A` as the only accent (deep `#A82D1B`; paper set `#F2EEEA`,
`#E4DED8`, `#C4351D`, `#16130F`). Code face — IBM Plex Mono, carrying every
technical string. Ratio — ground 82%, greys 14%, vermilion 4%. Finish — flat hard
vector, no gradients, no curves except where noted.

**Open.** Display face: turn 5 offers Bricolage Grotesque (5a), Sora (5b),
Libre Franklin (5c). Mark: turn 6 offers nine chanterelle marks (6a–6i) —
cluster, shelter, fan, chanterelle, spore ring, bell, wedge, plan, forks.

**Superseded.** Everything mineral: the cleaved-rhomb mark, the crystal-habit and
alchemical studies, and the Helvetica → Schibsted Grotesk drawn-C wordmark. The
drawn C is finished work (arms matched to the I's crossbar at 0.213 cap, gap at
the word's own 0.071 em) and survives in `Cinnabar Brand.dc.html` if the mushroom
direction is abandoned.
